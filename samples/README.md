# Audio samples

Put snoring recordings in `snoring_positive/`.

Put non-snoring recordings in `non_snoring_negative/`.

Good negative examples are just as important as snoring examples: silence, quiet breathing, talking, coughing, phone movement, bedding rustle, fan noise, air conditioning, traffic, and partner movement.

Recommended naming:

```text
2026-07-20-night1-snore-001.m4a
2026-07-20-night1-fan-negative-001.m4a
```

Avoid uploading private conversations unless they are truly needed and everyone recorded agreed.
